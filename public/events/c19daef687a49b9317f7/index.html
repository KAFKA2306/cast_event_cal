Mindful Communication

Mindful Communication is purposefully paying attention to our interactions with others without judgment. Listening with our full attention‚ presence‚ and empathy. Speaking with loving-kindness and assertiveness. Join us for an interactive‚ topic-based event where we explore how to bring more presence‚ clarity‚ and compassion into the ways we communicate. Each event features a specific communication topic such as ~ Boundaries ~ Active listening ~ Communication styles ~ Non-violent communication We will introduce these topics through an activity‚ short video‚ reading‚ or slides‚ followed by a guided group discussion. This is a space for learning‚ reflection‚ and connection through shared conversation. We aim to build skills and strategies we can use in our relationships and daily communication.
